# Finding a source that actually shows the interface

Read at procedure step 3, every time. Both rules here were learned by getting them wrong.

## Search brands, never categories

Uploaders title videos with a product's NAME. Almost nobody titles a video with its category, so a
category query matches the generic business-content cluster instead — dashboards, KPI explainers,
software comparisons.

```
CATEGORY  "capital de trabajo para bodegas peru app"     -> ~13 of 16 results were noise
BRAND     "Yape negocios prestamo como solicitar"
          "Mibanco app prestamo negocio peru"            -> ~5 of 18, with official channels
```

The competitor list IS the brand list. Use it — one spread per competitor, not one spread for the
category.

## Walkthrough beats spot, and the official channel is usually the spot

The counter-intuitive one. Channel authority tells you who made a video; it says nothing about
whether the video shows the product.

| Source | Typically publishes | Useful for screens |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Official brand channel | brand films, testimonials, animated explainers | rarely |
| Third-party tutorial channel | screen recordings of the real flow | usually |
| Government / trade body | concept animations | no |
| Conference talk | slides about the product | no |

Measured on a real run: of five official-channel videos, four were spots with zero product screens;
three third-party tutorials carried the entire flow. Prefer the tutorial.

Tells for a walkthrough, from the title: `cómo solicitar`, `paso a paso`, `tutorial`, `2025`,
`review`. Tells for a spot: a testimonial name, a slogan, a duration under two minutes.

## Record what the source does NOT show

After looking at the survey sheet, record the verdict with `source --kind walkthrough|spot|mixed`
and a `--shows` note. This is what later stops someone reading an empty cell as an absent feature:
a `spot` source explains every `not-in-source` gap for that competitor at once.

## Watch the side of the transaction

A brand can serve two audiences and its videos mix them. Searching a lending marketplace returns
mostly "how to INVEST" — the lender's side, not the borrower's. Before capturing, confirm the video
is on the side of the user your flow describes.

## Watch the country

Interfaces localise. A field labelled with the wrong country's ID document, a currency symbol that
does not match, a phone format from elsewhere — any of these means the source is a different market
build, and its screens do not belong in the grid. Note it and find another source.
